Here we go again! **S.E.P** has been selected at **Bloodtober Fest 2026**.

After Fantastic Gijón and Lloret Negre, that makes three festivals backing the film. Every new selection is a huge boost for the whole team, and it's a joy to see this story about an office where the unthinkable is handled like any other paperwork keep finding its audience.

Thanks again to the whole cast and crew. We'll keep sharing news here.
