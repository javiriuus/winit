That's a wrap! **Jornada Laboral**, my new short film, is in the can.

It was a long day outdoors in Madrid, under the trees with the city in the background, with Carlos García on camera and a crew who gave it everything from start to finish. Editing starts now, but I didn't want to wait to share the first stills.
