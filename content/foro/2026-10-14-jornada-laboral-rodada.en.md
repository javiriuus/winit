That's a wrap! **Jornada Laboral**, my new short film, is in the can.

> On a hot August day, Luis is waiting near a bench for a client who doesn't seem to be coming. Sitting there, he's interrupted by a boy who, full of curiosity and impatience, wants to know what he's doing. After much insistence, Luis reveals that his job is to let different clients beat him up in exchange for money.

Cinematography is by **Carlos García**, who was also the director of photography on S.E.P. Editing starts now, but I didn't want to wait to share the first stills.

## On set

Shooting outdoors has its charm, but it's also one of the hardest things to pull off: the sun moves, clouds come and go and the light changes from one shot to the next. On top of that, we shot without any lamps. All the light is sunlight, shaped only with mirrors and diffusers, chasing continuity take after take. Here are some photos from the day.
