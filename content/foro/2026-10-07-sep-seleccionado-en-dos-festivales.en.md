Good news: **S.E.P** has been selected at two festivals, **Fantastic Gijón** and **Lloret Negre**.

The whole team is thrilled. S.E.P started as a story about an office where the unthinkable is handled like any other paperwork, and seeing it reach two festivals is the best recognition for everyone who gave their all to the project.

Thank you to the whole cast and crew for believing in this story. We'll keep sharing news here.
