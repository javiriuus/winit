# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Three audiences who arrive to decide whether to hire Javier Llarena (confirmed, all three weighted):

- **Brands and agencies** looking for a director for campaigns and branded content.
- **Film production companies** looking for a director, director of photography or crew for shorts and fiction.
- **Music artists** looking for a director for music videos.

Their job on the site: judge the work quickly, understand what roles he can take on, and get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio site of Javier Llarena, filmmaker, creative director and storyteller. It shows selected work and the professional services he offers, and converts visitors into contact requests. Success is a visitor watching work and sending a message.

## Positioning

One person who covers the whole chain: direction, cinematography, editing, color, photography, production and screenwriting, with an engineering background applied to visual media.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 14 (pages router) + Tailwind CSS 3, deployed on Vercel. framer-motion and react-icons already installed.
- Contact form posts to `/api/contact` (nodemailer, Gmail credentials from env). Behavior must be kept.
- Catalog to preserve exactly: 9 services (Film Director, Director of Photography, Video Editor, Colorist, Photographer, Producer, Screenwriter, Industry Experience, Engineer) and 10 YouTube works (IDs and titles in the Work component).
- Bilingual site: Spanish and English with a language switch (confirmed).
- Credits / collaborations section stays out (placeholder content only).

## Brand Commitments

- **Javier Llarena** is the primary name. WinitMoney is secondary (repo/package name, logo file `public/logo.png`).
- Social: Instagram @javierllarenafilms, YouTube @javirius5294, LinkedIn profile (URLs in Header).

- Visual references named by the user as the bar to sit alongside: A24 (a24films.com) and Samba Films (sambafilms.com). Dark, image-led, minimal production-company register; the work and its titles carry the page.

## Evidence on Hand

- 10 YouTube works: S.E.P Shortfilm teaser, La belleza del caos, 3:08, Valdo – Me gustas más que dormir, Aún Recuerdo, TÓXICO, Te he vuelto a ver, PRENDE EL BLON, Y si...?, Miénteme Como Sabes.

- No testimonials, client logos, awards or press on hand. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The work leads; the interface steps back.
2. Every role on offer is real and backed by the reel; no invented claims.
3. Contact is always one step away.
4. Both languages are first-class, not a translation afterthought.
